Where to hire a social media manager

Last checked: Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · Discovery guide · Not a ranking

Page question. You need someone to plan, publish, and/or reply on social channels — and want the right hiring venue before you hand over account access.

Pick the venue by the job

Fitting guide (editorial judgment, not a ranking)
If you need…ConsiderBe careful when…
A month of scheduled posts from a template Marketplace packages with channel list, post count, and revision rules spelled out. They promise “viral growth” without a measurement plan.
Strategy + community management Open marketplace or direct hire with a trial month, clear KPIs, and separate creative vs reply SLAs. One person is expected to also run paid ads without ad-account experience in the portfolio.
X / Twitter-native growth See our dedicated hire an X (Twitter) marketer guide — different craft than Instagram/TikTok calendars. You hire a generic “social media” Gig for a reply-heavy X account.
Brand-safe ongoing presence Direct hire or curated network with reference checks and a written escalation path for comments. You share passwords instead of role-based access / business suites.

Portfolio and process checks

  • Case studies with channel + timeframe + what they controlled (organic vs paid).
  • Sample calendar and reply guidelines — not only pretty grids.
  • Tooling: native schedulers vs third-party; who owns the seats.
  • Red flag: guaranteed follower counts or engagement screenshots without context.

Brief checklist (social media manager)

  • Channels in scope; posting cadence; languages/time zones.
  • Brand voice samples; banned topics; escalation for complaints.
  • Access method (Meta Business, X delegated access) — never personal password sharing.
  • Reporting: metrics definitions, weekly vs monthly, who pulls ads data.
  • Content ownership and unused draft handoff at contract end.

Wage context

BLS does not publish a single “social media manager” OOH profile identical to marketplace titles. The closest broad OEWS category often used for marketing specialists is Market Research Analysts and Marketing Specialists (SOC 13-1161), with a May 2025 median around $78,760 in secondary compilations of OEWS — verify on BLS OEWS before you budget. Treat any employee median as context only; freelancers price retainers differently. For writing-heavy roles, see also BLS Writers and Authors median $76,910 (May 2025).

FAQ

Should social media managers get admin access on day one?

Prefer business-suite roles with least privilege, a start date, and a revocation checklist in the contract. Avoid sharing personal passwords.

Is this the same as hiring an ads manager?

Not necessarily. Organic management and paid media are different crafts. Scope ads separately if you need both.
