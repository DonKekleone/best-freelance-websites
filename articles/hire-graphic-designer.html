Where to hire a graphic designer

Last checked: Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · Discovery guide · Not a ranking

Page question. You need visual assets beyond a single logo — ads, one-pagers, packaging comps, social sets — and want to know which hiring venue shape fits before you post.

Pick the venue by the job

Fitting guide (editorial judgment, not a ranking)
If you need…ConsiderBe careful when…
A fixed pack of social or ad creatives Marketplace Gig / catalog listings with revision counts and file types listed. Brand system work or print production specs are vague in the Gig text.
Multiple concepts and a longer collaboration Open marketplaces (job post + proposals) with funded milestones per asset round. You evaluate on hourly rate alone without a portfolio match to your channel.
Senior designer for a campaign system Curated networks or direct outreach to designers whose case studies match your channel (print vs performance social). Budget assumes “one banner = full campaign.”
Someone you already trust Direct hire with a written brief and escrow/milestones. You skip funding and rely on “pay when done.”

Portfolio checks specific to graphic design

  • Samples in the same channel (Instagram crop vs print bleed vs packaging dieline).
  • Before/after or constraint stories (rebrand within existing colors, accessibility contrast).
  • File hygiene: layered source files, export presets, named styles — ask in the interview.
  • Red flag: only mockups with stock photos and no real client constraints explained.

Brief checklist (graphic design)

Start from the hiring brief template, then add:

  • Asset list with sizes/safe zones (e.g. 1080×1080, 1080×1920, print bleed).
  • Brand kit links (logo SVG, fonts, colors) or explicit “create interim kit” scope.
  • Copy ownership — who writes headlines; character limits.
  • Revision rounds per asset vs per campaign.
  • Final handoff: native files (AI/FIG/PSD), exports, and license for fonts/photos.

Cost context (employees, not freelancers)

BLS reports the median annual wage for graphic designers was $62,960 in May 2025 (~$30.27/hour). BLS wage surveys do not include most self-employed freelancers, so marketplace quotes can sit well above or below that employee median after tax, tools, and unbilled time. Use it as a sanity floor, not a price list. See also what graphic design work costs.

FAQ

Is a logo designer the same as a graphic designer?

Often overlapping skills, but logo/identity work is a narrower brief. If you only need a mark, start with our logo designer guide. If you need ongoing ads, decks, or packaging, hire for graphic design with an asset list.

Should I use a design contest?

Contests can produce many concepts quickly but may optimize for speed over brand systems or print production. Read IP transfer terms before funding a prize.
