Where to hire an SEO specialist

Portfolio checks specific to SEO

  • Before/after for metrics they controlled (organic sessions, indexed pages) — not vanity screenshots alone.
  • Sample audit outline; tooling named (crawl, log, Search Console).
  • Red flag: buying links or PBNs as the primary tactic without disclosure.

Brief checklist

  • Primary locales/languages; competitors; CMS; what is out of scope (ads, PR).
  • Access: Search Console user, analytics viewer, staging — not shared passwords.
  • Deliverables: audit PDF, ticket list, content briefs count, reporting cadence.

Closest broad BLS OEWS category often used for search marketing specialists is Market Research Analysts and Marketing Specialists (SOC 13-1161), May 2025 median about $78,760 in secondary OEWS compilations — verify on BLS; employee wages ≠ freelance retainers.

FAQ

Is SEO the same as running Google Ads?

No. SEO is organic search work; paid search is a separate craft. Scope them separately.

Should I share wp-admin as admin on day one?

Prefer least-privilege roles and a revocation checklist. Avoid personal password sharing.
