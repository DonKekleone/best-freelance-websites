Where to hire a UI/UX designer (and how to brief them)

Updated Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · Discovery guide · Wage context from U.S. BLS

“UI/UX designer” covers three different jobs: research (interviews, usability tests), interaction/UX design (flows, wireframes, prototypes), and UI/visual design (screens, components, design systems). Name which one you need first.

Portfolio checklist

  • Case studies that show the problem, process and outcome, not just Dribbble shots.
  • Work in your platform (web app, iOS, Android) and density (dashboard vs consumer).
  • Editable source files in your tool (e.g. Figma) with components, not flattened images.
  • Accessibility awareness: contrast, focus states, text sizing.
  • Evidence of developer handoff: specs, tokens, redlines or dev-mode notes.

Brief checklist (design-specific)

Start from the hiring brief template, then add:

  • Users and the top 3 tasks they must complete.
  • Existing screens, analytics or support tickets that show the problem.
  • Number of flows/screens, breakpoints, and states (empty, error, loading).
  • Who builds it: link to your developer or hire a web developer.
  • File ownership and license terms: see the freelance contract checklist.

Red flags

  • Portfolio pieces that are concept redesigns of famous apps only, with no client work explained.
  • Refusal to share editable files at handoff.
  • Fixed quote for an “entire app” with no screen or flow count.

Wage context

The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reports a median annual wage of $104,000 for web and digital interface designers in May 2025 (lowest 10% under $53,750; highest 10% over $201,550).

BLS wage figures describe employees in May 2025. BLS states its wage survey does not include self-employed workers, so freelance quotes can sit well above or below these numbers (freelancers cover their own tax, tools, benefits and unbilled time). Use them as a sanity floor for context, not a price list.

FAQ

What is the difference between a UI and a UX designer?

UX work focuses on research, flows and usability; UI work focuses on visual screens, components and design systems. Many freelancers do both, but portfolios usually show a stronger side.

What do UI/UX designers earn in the U.S.?

BLS reports a May 2025 median annual wage of $104,000 for web and digital interface designers (employees). Freelance quotes vary widely and are not covered by that survey.
