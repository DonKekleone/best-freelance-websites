Costs · Writing

What copywriting work costs (context, not a price list)

Last checked: Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · BLS employee wages · Not a freelance rate card

Page question. How should a buyer sanity-check copywriting quotes without inventing “average Gig prices”?
BLS scope. Writers and Authors wage data covers employees in that occupation. It is not a freelance copywriting rate survey. Self-employed specialists (CRO landing pages, email flows, technical white papers) often price far from the employee median.

BLS anchor (May 2025)

  • Median annual wage for writers and authors: $76,910.
  • Use the OOH profile for the definition of the occupation and pay percentiles.

Source: U.S. BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook, Writers and Authors (checked Nov 5, 2026).

Budgeting tips that beat “per word” folklore

  1. Scope deliverables: page list, SEO constraints, interview time, revision rounds.
  2. Separate research-heavy thought leadership from short product blurbs.
  3. Add marketplace client fees via the fee comparison.
  4. Hire using where to hire a copywriter venue fit — not cost alone.

FAQ

Is $1/word a real market rate?

It is a folklore number, not a BLS statistic. Price from scoped deliverables and samples, then compare proposals.

Does the writers median include marketing copywriters?

OOH Writers and Authors is a broad occupation. Many marketing copy roles may be classified differently by employers. Treat the figure as context only.
