Where to hire a freelance bookkeeper

Updated Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · Discovery guide · Wage context from U.S. BLS

Scope first. Bookkeeping (recording and reconciling transactions) is not the same as accounting advice or tax preparation. If you need tax filing or advice, look for appropriately credentialed professionals; a bookkeeper may hand off to them.

Match the job to the venue

Access and security rules

  • Invite the bookkeeper as a separate user in your accounting software; never share your own login.
  • Give bank access as read-only feeds or exports, not transfer permissions.
  • Never send full card numbers or passwords in chat.
  • Remove access the day the engagement ends.

Brief checklist

  • Entity type, accounting software, and basis (cash or accrual) if known.
  • Number of bank, card and payment-processor accounts; rough monthly transaction count.
  • Months to catch up and the deadline (e.g. before your tax preparer needs the books).
  • Deliverables: reconciled accounts, monthly P&L and balance sheet, notes on unclear items.
  • Confidentiality expectations in writing: see the contract checklist.

Red flags

  • Asking for your bank login or authority to move money.
  • Promising tax savings or “IRS-proof” books.
  • No questions about volume or accounts before quoting.

Wage context

BLS reports a median annual wage of $50,670 ($24.36/hour) for bookkeeping, accounting and auditing clerks in May 2025 (lowest 10% under $36,000; highest 10% over $74,550). BLS also notes software automation is shifting the role toward analysis and advisory work.

BLS wage figures describe employees in May 2025. BLS states its wage survey does not include self-employed workers, so freelance quotes can sit well above or below these numbers (freelancers cover their own tax, tools, benefits and unbilled time). Use them as a sanity floor for context, not a price list.

FAQ

Is a bookkeeper the same as an accountant?

No. Bookkeepers record, classify and reconcile transactions and produce reports; accounting advice and tax preparation are separate services that may require credentials.

What do bookkeepers earn in the U.S.?

BLS reports a May 2025 median of $50,670 per year ($24.36/hour) for bookkeeping, accounting and auditing clerks (employees, not freelancers).
