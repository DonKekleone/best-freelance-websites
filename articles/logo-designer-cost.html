What logo design work costs (context, not a price list)

Last checked: Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · BLS employee wages

No official “average logo price.” BLS does not publish a logo-only rate. We use the graphic designers occupation as context and push you to scope deliverables.

BLS context (May 2025)

  • Graphic designers median annual wage: $62,960 (~$30.27/hour).

Source: BLS OOH Graphic Designers (checked Nov 5, 2026). Self-employed freelancers are largely outside OEWS.

What changes the quote more than the median

  • Wordmark vs custom lettering vs full identity system.
  • Number of concepts and revision rounds.
  • Trademark search responsibility (usually yours / your attorney).
  • Marketplace client fees — fee comparison.

Venue fit: where to hire a logo designer. Sibling cost page: graphic designer cost.

FAQ

Is a $5 logo Gig enough?

Sometimes for throwaway marks; rarely for trademark-sensitive brands. Scope IP, source files, and revisions before you optimize for price.

Should I use BLS to set a freelancer’s hourly rate?

No. Use it as a sanity floor for employee wages, then price the project from deliverables.
