Costs · Video

What video editing work costs (context, not a price list)

Last checked: Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · BLS employee wages

BLS scope. Film and video editor wages describe employees. Freelancers price projects covering gear, plugins, revisions, and unbilled coordination — often far from a simple hourly conversion of the median.

BLS anchor (May 2025)

  • Film and video editors median annual wage: $75,420.
  • Lowest 10% less than $40,280; highest 10% more than $148,550.

Source: U.S. BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook, Film and Video Editors and Camera Operators (checked Nov 5, 2026).

Budgeting tips

  1. Scope runtime, deliverable aspect ratios, revision rounds, and who supplies music/fonts licenses.
  2. Separate simple assembly cuts from motion-graphics-heavy packages.
  3. Add marketplace client fees via the fee comparison.
  4. Hire using where to hire a video editor.

FAQ

Can I budget $36/hour because BLS implies that?

Not safely. Convert employee medians only as rough context, then collect fixed proposals against a written cut list.

Do you publish average Fiverr video edit prices?

No. Those fluctuate and are not official statistics.
