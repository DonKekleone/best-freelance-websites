Where to hire a virtual assistant (and what to delegate)

Updated Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · Discovery guide · Wage context from U.S. BLS

A virtual assistant works best on repeatable tasks you can document: inbox triage, scheduling, data entry, research lists, customer replies from templates. If you cannot write the steps down, the VA cannot repeat them.

Delegate first vs keep

Starting point (editorial judgment)
Good first tasksKeep or delegate later
Calendar scheduling and meeting prepAnything that moves money
Inbox labeling and draft replies from templatesHiring/firing decisions and sensitive HR
Lead or vendor research into a spreadsheetLegal or contract commitments
CRM / data entry cleanupPasswords to primary accounts

Match the job to the venue

  • One-off task bundles: marketplace Gigs or fixed-price posts with a clear output (e.g. “200 leads in this format”).
  • Ongoing part-time help: hourly contracts with a weekly cap; see hourly vs fixed-price.
  • Executive-level support: experienced assistants via direct hire or specialist agencies; expect higher rates.

Run a paid trial task

  1. Pick one real, low-risk task (2–3 hours).
  2. Write the steps and the definition of done.
  3. Pay for it; judge accuracy, questions asked, and communication.
  4. Turn their notes into your SOP before scaling.

Access rules

  • Use delegated access (shared calendars, delegated inbox, separate seats) instead of sharing your password.
  • Use a password manager’s sharing feature where possible, and turn on two-factor authentication.
  • No payment authority in the first weeks.

Red flags

  • Asks to be paid off-platform right away (why that is risky).
  • Wants remote control of your computer through tools like AnyDesk or TeamViewer early on.
  • Cannot explain how they handle time zones and response times.

Wage context

BLS reports a median annual wage of $48,310 ($23.23/hour) for secretaries and administrative assistants in May 2025; executive secretaries and executive administrative assistants had a median of $76,590. BLS notes some administrative assistants work from home as virtual assistants.

BLS wage figures describe employees in May 2025. BLS states its wage survey does not include self-employed workers, so freelance quotes can sit well above or below these numbers (freelancers cover their own tax, tools, benefits and unbilled time). Use them as a sanity floor for context, not a price list.

FAQ

What should I delegate to a virtual assistant first?

Repeatable, documented, low-risk tasks such as scheduling, inbox triage with templates, research lists and data entry. Keep money movement and legal commitments with you at first.

What do administrative assistants earn in the U.S.?

BLS reports a May 2025 median of $48,310 per year for secretaries and administrative assistants and $76,590 for executive assistants (employees, not freelancers).
