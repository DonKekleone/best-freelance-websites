Costs · Design

What UI/UX design work costs (context, not a price list)

Last checked: Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · BLS employee wages

Occupation mismatch risk. Marketplace “UI/UX” titles span research, wireframes, and high-fidelity UI. BLS splits web and digital interface designers (median $104,000) and web developers ($92,650) in May 2025 — neither is a freelance product-design survey.

BLS anchors (May 2025)

  • Web and digital interface designers: median $104,000.
  • Web developers: median $92,650.
  • Combined OOH “Web Developers and Digital Designers” headline median: $99,520.

Source: BLS OOH Web Developers and Digital Designers (checked Nov 5, 2026).

Scope before you compare quotes

  1. Research vs UI-only; number of screens; design system vs one-off screens.
  2. Handoff format (Figma library, specs, redlines).
  3. Marketplace fees via fee comparison.
  4. Hire guide: where to hire a UI/UX designer.

FAQ

Why is this higher than graphic designer median?

Different BLS occupations. Interface/digital design roles sit in computer occupations with higher OEWS medians than graphic designers ($62,960).

Can I convert $104k to an hourly freelance rate?

Not directly. Freelancers add overhead and price by project risk. Use proposals against a screen list.
