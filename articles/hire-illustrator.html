Portfolio checks

  • Same medium (vector vs painted vs 3D) as your brief.
  • Process: sketch → color → finals, not only polished finals.
  • Licensing clarity: exclusive vs non-exclusive; AI-training clauses if that matters to you.

Brief checklist

  • Use cases (print, app, merch); color limits; reference board.
  • Deliverables: layered source, exports, and who owns the character IP.
  • Revision rounds per stage (sketch vs color).

FAQ

Is an illustrator the same as a graphic designer?

Overlapping skills, different deliverables. Illustration is image-making; graphic design often includes layout systems, type, and multi-asset campaigns.

Who owns the character after delivery?

Only what your contract says. Spell out exclusive rights, merch rights, and derivative works before funding.
