How much does a freelance web developer cost?

Updated Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · Cost guide · Benchmarks from U.S. BLS and official platform fee pages

Honest answer: there is no reliable single price. We do not publish “average freelance rates” scraped from listings because they are not verifiable. Instead, this page gives you official benchmarks and the levers that move a quote.

What drives the quote

  • Scope: number of templates/pages, integrations (payments, forms, CRM), content migration.
  • Stack: site builder or WordPress theme vs custom front end vs web app with a back end.
  • Who supplies design and copy: a developer working from finished designs is a different job.
  • Seniority and location of the freelancer.
  • Ongoing work: hosting, updates, security patches, and support after launch.

BLS states its wage survey does not include self-employed workers, so freelance quotes can sit well above or below these numbers (freelancers cover their own tax, tools, benefits and unbilled time). Use them as a sanity floor for context, not a price list.

Add platform fees to the budget

If you hire through a marketplace, the client-side fee is on top of the freelancer’s price. Examples from official fee pages (checked Nov 5, 2026): Freelancer.com employer fee 3% or $3 (whichever is greater); Fiverr buyer service fee 5.5% plus $3.50 on purchases under $200; Upwork client marketplace fee varies by plan. Details: fee comparison.

Worked budget (illustrative arithmetic, not a quote)

Suppose you collect three written quotes for a 5-page marketing site and the middle one is $2,000 fixed-price:

  • Via Freelancer.com: + 3% employer fee = $2,060, before payment-method fees.
  • Via a Fiverr Gig at the same price: + 5.5% = $2,110 (the $3.50 add-on applies only under $200).
  • Add a contingency of 10–20% for change requests you have not thought of yet (our editorial rule of thumb).

How to get comparable quotes

  • Send the same written brief to every candidate (template).
  • Ask for a milestone breakdown, not just a total.
  • Ask what is excluded: content entry, stock images, hosting, post-launch fixes.
  • Fund milestones through the platform’s escrow: how escrow works.

FAQ

How much does a freelance web developer cost?

It depends on scope, stack, seniority and ongoing support. For context, BLS reports a May 2025 median annual wage of $92,650 for employed web developers; freelancers set their own rates and marketplace fees are added on top.

Do I pay platform fees on top of a developer's quote?

Usually yes as the client. For example Freelancer.com charges employers 3% or $3, and Fiverr charges buyers 5.5% plus $3.50 under $200 (official pages checked Nov 5, 2026).
