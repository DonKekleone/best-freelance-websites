Where to hire a logo designer (and when not to use each option)

Updated Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · Discovery guide · Fee details link out to Platforms research

Start with the job shape: a single mark for a side project is a different hire than a trademark-sensitive brand system with guidelines. The venue should match the risk, not the lowest sticker price.

Match the job to the venue

Practical fitting guide (editorial judgment, not a ranking)
If you need…ConsiderBe careful when…
A packaged logo Gig with clear tiers Marketplace Gig platforms (e.g. Fiverr) where scope, revisions, and price are listed up front. Order is prepaid. You need deep strategy, many stakeholders, or custom illustration beyond the Gig text.
Proposals from many freelancers Open marketplaces (e.g. Upwork, Freelancer.com) where you post a job and compare proposals. Fund milestones before work. Your brief is vague — you will optimize for price, not fit.
Pre-vetted senior designers quickly Curated networks (e.g. Toptal) with matching and trial periods. Ask for a written quote; there is no public logo rate card on Toptal’s FAQ. Budget is small or the work is a one-hour tweak.
Many concepts fast via competition Contest-style sites (including Freelancer contests). Prize funding rules vary. Trademark, originality, or long-term brand systems matter — contest dynamics can reward speed over craft. Read each platform’s IP transfer terms carefully before you fund a prize.
Someone you already trust, paid in milestones Direct hire with a written brief + funded milestones (marketplace escrow or a direct escrow workflow). You skip funding and rely on “pay when done.”

Minimum brief for a logo hire

Copy the longer template on our hiring brief page. For logos specifically, include:

  • Business name, tagline (if any), and industries to avoid resembling.
  • Where the mark will live (app icon, signage, embroidery) — this changes geometry.
  • Must-have / must-not visual directions; 3–5 reference links with notes.
  • Deliverables: primary mark, single-color, favicon/app icon crop, file types (SVG, PDF, PNG).
  • Revision rounds and trademark responsibility (you should run legal checks; freelancers are not your trademark counsel).

Fees and payment protection

Do not budget from memory. Use the sourced table on Best Freelance Platforms — fee comparison (last checked Nov 5, 2026) and the escrow / milestones guide.

Headline examples already sourced there include Fiverr’s buyer service fee (5.5% + $3.50 under $200) and freelancers earning 80% of purchase amount; Upwork client marketplace fees up to 7.99% on Basic (with stated bank discounts); Freelancer.com employer 3% / freelancer 10% project fees. Re-check official pages before you hire.
