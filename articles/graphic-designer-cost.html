Costs · Design

What graphic design work costs (context, not a price list)

Last checked: Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · BLS employee wages · Not a freelance rate card

Page question. Roughly what should a small business expect to budget for graphic design help — without inventing marketplace “average prices”?
Important. BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics describe employees. The survey does not cover most self-employed freelancers. Freelance quotes must cover taxes, benefits, tools, revisions, and unbilled sales time — so they often exceed a simple hourly conversion of the employee median.

BLS anchor (May 2025)

  • Median annual wage for graphic designers: $62,960 (~$30.27/hour).
  • Lowest 10%: less than $39,520; highest 10%: more than $104,910.
  • Specialized design services industry median (same OOH table): $63,780.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook, Graphic Designers (page visited/checked Nov 5, 2026; OOH cites May 2025 wages).

How to turn that into a project budget

  1. List assets and rounds (e.g. 8 social posts, 2 revisions each).
  2. Ask proposers for hours or fixed milestones tied to that list — not a vague “campaign.”
  3. Add marketplace client fees from the fee comparison (do not guess).
  4. Hold a contingency for stock licenses and font licensing if your brand kit is incomplete.

FAQ

Can I budget $30/hour because BLS says so?

Not safely. $30.27 is the May 2025 employee median hourly wage. Freelancers price projects differently. Use BLS as context, then collect real proposals against a written asset list.

Do you publish average Fiverr or Upwork graphic design prices?

No. Those fluctuate and are not official statistics. We link fee schedules, not invented averages.
